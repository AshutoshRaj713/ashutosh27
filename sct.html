📘 Why School Needs a Website?

In today’s digital era, students and parents expect information online.

A school website works as a **central digital notice board** – accessible anytime, anywhere.

📂 Offline Material ➜ Online Access

• Notices & circulars online (no paper loss)
• Notes & syllabus available 24×7
• Important questions & study material
• Results & announcements instantly
• Holiday list & exam schedules

🎓 Student Benefits

• Students never miss important updates
• Easy revision from notes & questions
• Learning continues even outside classroom
• Organized and stress-free access to study material

🏫 School & Staff Benefits

• Less manual work for teachers
• One-time upload, multiple reach
• Professional digital presence
• Improved communication with parents
